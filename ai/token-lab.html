What’s happening here? ＋

Each token is a piece of text with a unique integer ID. In Whole words mode, words (including internal apostrophes) and punctuation are looked up individually. In Longest match mode, the longest vocabulary entry at the current position wins; unmatched characters become individual unknown tokens. Spaces are skipped unless included inside a matched token. Matching ignores case by default. Unknown pieces use the reserved ID 0.

This is a teaching tokenizer. Production tokenizers often learn subword vocabularies with methods such as BPE and may encode whitespace too. The IDs here are your own dictionary, not a specific model’s IDs.
